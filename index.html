How can I place an order?
Simply add your desired items to your cart, fill in your name or handle in the bag, and tap "Order & Confirm Payment via Telegram" or "Order & Confirm Payment via WhatsApp". Your order note will automatically copy and open a direct chat with me to finalize PayNow details.

Yes! We provide doorstep local delivery across Singapore. You can input your preferred delivery address or postal code directly inside your checkout note.

We accept PayNow / FAST bank transfers in Singapore. Once you submit your order via Telegram or WhatsApp, I will provide the PayNow QR or number for convenient transfer.

Yes, custom dimensions and tailored orders can be arranged! You can place a Custom Order directly on the storefront with your notes, or tap "Contact me here" to discuss.
