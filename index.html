Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions or need help?

Contact me here
01 How do I place an order?

Add your desired items to the bag, provide your name or Telegram handle and delivery address in the cart drawer, and tap "Order & Confirm Payment via Telegram". A formatted order note will open directly with @e1yse on Telegram where you can confirm and receive PayNow details.

02 What are the available collection or delivery options?

We operate exclusively on local doorstep delivery within Singapore. Simply specify your delivery address and postal code during checkout, and courier delivery arrangements will be confirmed directly with you on Telegram.

03 Can I request bespoke customizations?

Yes, custom scales, layered color palettes, and bespoke designs can be requested! Tap "Contact me here" or message @e1yse directly on Telegram to discuss your custom project.

04 How do I arrange PayNow payment?

When your order note is submitted to Telegram, you will be provided with the registered PayNow QR / mobile number. Once payment is made and verified via screenshot in chat, your order will be prepared and dispatched for delivery.
