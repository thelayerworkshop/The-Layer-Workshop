How do I place an order and make payment?

Simply add your chosen items to the cart, fill in your contact information and notes, then tap "Order & Confirm Payment via Telegram". This generates your order receipt with a unique Order ID and opens your Telegram chat directly with us with the complete message pre-filled. You just hit Send, and payment details (PayNow / bank transfer) will be shared directly to confirm your order!

What are the fulfillment options?

We operate on a local delivery only basis within Singapore. You may choose between:
• Self-Collection @ Redhill MRT: Complimentary meetup by appointment timing scheduled via Telegram.
• Local Delivery (Singapore Only): Doorstep delivery within Singapore. Delivery fees depend on your postal code and are confirmed together with your order in Telegram. (We do not offer international shipping).

Are items handmade or made-to-order?

All items are designed and handcrafted with precision right here in Singapore by The Layer Workshop. Due to the handmade nature, slight organic textures make each piece unique. Ready-stock availability and crafting lead times will be confirmed right away in Telegram.

Can I request custom designs, colors, or variations?

Yes! We love custom projects. Whether you'd like a specific dimension, custom color tone, or bulk quantities for gifting, reach out to us directly on Telegram at @e1yse and we'll see how we can bring your idea to life.
